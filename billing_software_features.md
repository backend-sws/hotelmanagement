## BillKaro — Complete Billing Software

## Features Guide: Kya Hai, Kaise Use Karein, Kaise Kaam Karta Hai

Ye software gitti/ballu dealer, construction contractor, aur interior designer — teeno ke

liye design kiya gaya hai.

## SECTION 1 — BILLING & INVOICE

## 1.1 Sales Invoice

Kya hai? Customer ko maal ya service bechne ke baad jo bill diya jaata hai — usse Sales Invoice

kehte hain. Ye GST ke saath valid legal document hota hai.

## Kise use karna chahiye?

- Gitti/ballu dealer jo customer ko maal beche

- Interior designer jo client ko service bill kare

- Construction contractor jo client se payment maange

## Kaise use karein?

- 1. Dashboard mein "New Invoice" button dabao

- 2. Customer ka naam search karo ya naya add karo

- 3. Items add karo — naam, quantity, unit (ton/cft/nos/sqft), rate

- 4. GST rate choose karo (0% / 5% / 12% / 18% / 28%)

- 5. Discount chahiye toh woh bhi enter karo

- 6. Save karo → PDF download karo → WhatsApp pe share karo

## Kaise kaam karta hai?

- Customer select hote hi uski previous outstanding bhi dikhti hai

- Item enter karte hi rate auto-fill hota hai (jo aapne Item Master mein set kiya tha)

- GST automatically calculate hota hai - CGST + SGST ya IGST (state ke hisaab se)

- Invoice number automatically sequence mein badhta hai (INV-001, INV-002...)

- Har invoice save hone par customer ka ledger auto-update hota hai

## Example:

Ram Gitti Supplier ne Suresh Builder ko 50 ton gitti becha 800/ton par, 18% GST ke ₹ saath. System ne khud calculate kiya: ₹ 40,000 + ₹ 7,200 GST = ₹ 47,200 total.


## 1.2 Purchase Bill

Kya hai? Jab aap kisi supplier se maal khareedte ho — cement, rod, gitti, tiles — uska record

Purchase Bill mein hota hai.

## Kise use karna chahiye?

- Jo log supplier se khareedkar aage bechte hain (trader/dealer)

- Constructor jo material khareedta hai site ke liye

- Interior designer jo client ke liye material khareedta hai

## Kaise use karein?

- 1. "New Purchase" open karo

- 2. Supplier ka naam select karo

- 3. Items enter karo jo khareed rahe ho

- 4. Bill number (supplier ka) aur date enter karo

- 5. Amount enter karo → Save karo

## Kaise kaam karta hai?

- Purchase save hote hi stock automatically badh jaata hai

- Supplier ka payable ledger update hota hai (aapko kitna dena hai)

- GST Input Tax Credit (ITC) automatically track hota hai

- Baad mein jab payment karein toh wahan se link ho jaata hai

## 1.3 Proforma Invoice

Kya hai? Actual invoice se pehle customer ko jo estimate ya pre-bill diya jaata hai. Payment demand nahi hoti — sirf approval ke liye hota hai.

## Kise use karna chahiye?

- Interior designer jo client ko kaam shuru karne se pehle estimate de

- Constructor jo project quote de raha ho

- Dealer jise bulk order confirm karwana ho pehle

## Kaise use karein?

- 1. "New Proforma Invoice" open karo

- 2. Bilkul invoice ki tarah fill karo

- 3. Client ko share karo (WhatsApp/Email)

- 4. Client approve kare → "Convert to Invoice" button dabao → Real invoice ban jaayegi


## Kaise kaam karta hai?

- Proforma se stock mein koi change nahi aata

- Ledger pe bhi koi entry nahi hoti jab tak convert na ho

- Convert karte waqt sab data automatically transfer hota hai - dobara type nahi karna

## 1.4 Delivery Challan

Kya hai? Maal bhejna record karne ke liye — lekin abhi invoice nahi banana. Jaise gitti truck pe

load ki, challan de diya — invoice baad mein banega.

## Kise use karna chahiye?

- Gitti/ballu dealer jab truck nikalta hai

- Jab ek hi delivery pe kai trips hoti hain aur ek invoice baad mein banana hai

- Construction site pe material supply ke waqt

## Kaise use karein?

- 1. "New Delivery Challan" open karo

- 2. Customer/site ka naam enter karo

- 3. Material select karo (gitti 20mm / ballu fine / sand etc.)

- 4. Quantity enter karo

- 5. Truck number, driver naam enter karo

- 6. Save karo → Print karo → Driver ke haath challan do

- 7. Baad mein: Select challan → "Convert to Invoice"

## Kaise kaam karta hai?

- Challan save hone par stock kam hota hai (maal godown se gaya)

- Invoice tab tak nahi banta jab tak convert na karo

- Ek ya kai challans ko milaakar ek invoice bana sakte ho (monthly billing)

- Transport history track hoti hai - kaun driver, kaunsa truck, kab gaya

## 1.5 Credit Note / Debit Note

## Kya hai?

- Credit Note: Jab customer maal wapas kare ya invoice mein galti ho - customer ka balance reduce karo

- Debit Note: Jab supplier ko kuch extra charge karna ho ya woh maal wapas bheje

## Kaise use karein?


- 1. Original invoice search karo

- 2. "Create Credit Note" dabao

- 3. Return kiya hua item aur quantity enter karo

- 4. Reason likhao (damaged / wrong item / rate correction)

- 5. Save karo

## Kaise kaam karta hai?

- Credit note se customer ka outstanding amount reduce hota hai

- Wapas aaya maal stock mein add ho jaata hai automatically

- GST bhi adjust hota hai ‒ return ki GST credit wapas milti hai

## 1.6 Quotation

Kya hai? Customer ya client ko project ka estimate dena — kitna material lagega, kitni labour,

kitna total kharcha.

## Kise use karna chahiye?

- Interior designer jo naye client ko project proposal de

- Contractor jo tender ya work order ke liye quote de

- Dealer jo bulk order pe special rate de

## Kaise use karein?

- 1. "New Quotation" open karo

- 2. Client ka naam aur project details

- 3. Item by item estimate enter karo

- 4. Validity date set karo (e.g., valid for 30 days)

- 5. Terms & conditions likhao

- 6. PDF share karo client ko

- 7. Client accept kare → "Convert to Invoice" ya "Convert to Order"

## 1.7 Recurring Invoice

Kya hai? Jo invoice har mahine automatically generate ho — monthly rent, AMC, maintenance charges ke liye.

## Kaise use karein?

- 1. Ek invoice banao normally

- 2. "Set as Recurring" option check karo


- 3. Frequency choose karo: Weekly / Monthly / Quarterly

- 4. Start date aur end date (optional) set karo

- 5. Save karo — system automatically generate karta rahega

## Kaise kaam karta hai?

- Decide ki hui date pe invoice auto-create hoti hai

- Customer ko notification jaati hai (WhatsApp/SMS)

- Aapko kuch bhi nahi karna - sab automatic

## 1.8 E-Invoice & E-Waybill

## Kya hai?

- E-Invoice: ₹ 5 crore+ turnover wale businesses ke liye GST portal pe invoice register karna compulsory hai

- E-Waybill: ¹ 50,000+ ka maal ek jagah se doosri jagah bhejne par government ka permission document

## Kaise use karein?

- 1. Normal invoice banao

- 2. "Generate E-Invoice" button dabao

- 3. System GST portal se connect karke IRN number generate karta hai

- 4. QR code automatically invoice pe print hota hai

- 5. E-Waybill ke liye: Transporter details enter karo → Generate karo

## Kaise kaam karta hai?

- Software GST portal ke API se directly connect hota hai

- IRN (Invoice Reference Number) ek unique code hota hai

- Koi bhi scanner se QR code scan karke invoice verify kar sakta hai

- Gitti/construction ke bade orders mein E-Waybill zaroori hoti hai

## SECTION 2 — PARTY & CUSTOMER MANAGEMENT

## 2.1 Customer / Party Ledger

Kya hai? Har ek customer ka poora hisaab — kab kya kharida, kitna diya, kitna baaki hai — ek jagah.

## Kaise use karein?


- 1. "Parties" section open karo

- 2. Customer ka naam search karo

- 3. Uska ledger khulega — saari entries dikhegi

- 4. "Statement" button se PDF download karo

- 5. WhatsApp pe share karo customer ko

## Kaise kaam karta hai?

- Har invoice, payment, credit note - sab automatically ledger mein aata hai

- Balance always updated rehta hai

- "Send Statement" se customer ko apna hisaab khud check karne ko bhej sakte ho

- Confirmation ke baad payment mein disagreement nahi hota

## 2.2 Supplier Management

Kya hai? Gitti supplier, cement dealer, hardware shop — sabka record. Aapka kitna dena baaki hai

— sab track hota hai.

## Kaise use karein?

- 1. "Suppliers" section mein jaao

- 2. Supplier add karo — naam, GST number, phone, bank details

- 3. Har purchase automatically link hoti hai

- 4. Payment karo → "Record Payment" → Balance update

## 2.3 Outstanding Report

Kya hai? Ek report jisme dikhta hai kaun-kaun customer abhi tak paise nahi diya, aur kitna dena

baaki hai.

## Kaise use karein?

- 1. Reports → Outstanding

- 2. Filter: Customer-wise ya Supplier-wise

- 3. Filter: Amount ke hisaab se ya kitne din se baaki hai

- 4. Select karke "Send Reminder" dabao

## Kaise kaam karta hai?

- 0-30 days, 30-60 days, 60-90 days, 90+ days ke brackets mein dikhta hai

- Jo zyada purana hoga woh red mein highlight hoga

- Ek click mein WhatsApp reminder bhej sakte ho


## 2.4 Payment Reminder

Kya hai? Jo customer bhuul jaate hain ya delay karte hain unhe auto WhatsApp/SMS reminder

jaata hai.

## Kaise use karein?

- 1. Settings → Reminder

- 2. Template banao: "Dear {customer}, aapka {amount} ka payment {days} din se baaki ₹ hai..."

- 3. Days set karo: 7 din baad, 15 din baad, 30 din baad

- 4. Auto-send ya manual approve choose karo

## 2.5 Multiple Price List

Kya hai? Alag-alag customer categories ke liye alag rates. Thok wale ko kam, retail wale ko zyada.

## Kaise use karein?

- 1. Items → Price List 2. "Retail", "Wholesale", "Contractor" categories banao 3. Har category mein item ke alag rates set karo 4. Customer banate waqt uski category assign karo

- 5. Invoice banate waqt automatic sahi rate aayega

## SECTION 3 — STOCK & INVENTORY

## 3.1 Item / Product Master

Kya hai? Aapke business mein jo bhi maal ya service hai — sabka ek master record. Naam, unit, rate, GST — sab ek jagah set karo.

## Kaise use karein?

- 1. Items → "Add New Item" 2. Item naam: "Gitti 20mm", "Ballu Fine", "OPC Cement", "Vitrified Tiles 60x60" 3. Unit choose karo: Ton / CFT / Brass / Bag / Sqft / Nos / Rft 4. Sale rate aur Purchase rate enter karo 5. HSN Code enter karo (GST ke liye)

- 6. GST rate set karo


- 7. Opening stock enter karo

## Construction/Gitti ke liye khaas units:

- Brass = 100 cubic feet (locally use hoti hai)

- CFT = cubic feet

- Ton = metric ton (1000 kg)

- Sqft = square feet (interior ke liye)

## 3.2 Stock Summary

Kya hai? Aaj ke din aapke paas kitna maal hai — item-wise real-time stock.

## Kaise use karein?

- 1. Inventory → Stock Summary

- 2. Item-wise current stock dikhega

- 3. Low stock items red mein highlight honge

- 4. Filter by category (Aggregate / Cement / Tiles etc.)

## Kaise kaam karta hai?

- Har purchase mein stock badhta hai

- Har sale/delivery challan mein stock ghatata hai

- System automatically calculate karta rehta hai

- Low stock alert set kar sakte ho (e.g., gitti 20mm < 100 ton par alert)

## 3.3 Stock Transfer (Site to Site)

Kya hai? Ek site ya godown se doosri site pe maal bhejna — record ke saath.

## Kise use karna chahiye?

- Contractor jinke paas multiple sites hain

- Dealer jiske paas warehouse aur yard alag hain

## Kaise use karein?

- 1. Inventory → Stock Transfer

- 2. "From" location choose karo

- 3. "To" location choose karo

- 4. Items aur quantity enter karo

- 5. Save → Transfer slip print karo


## 3.4 Material Consumption (Project-wise)

Kya hai? Kisi project ya site mein kitna material use hua — costing ke liye track karo.

## Kaise use karein?

- 1. Project open karo

- 2. "Add Consumption" dabao

- 3. Item aur quantity mark karo jitna use hua

- 4. System project ki cost calculate karta rahega

## Kaise kaam karta hai?

- Stock se item reduce hota hai

- Project mein cost add hoti hai

- Baad mein project-wise profit/loss report mein dikhta hai

## 3.5 Godown / Multi-location

Kya hai? Alag-alag physical locations ka alag stock maintain karo.

## Kaise use karein?

- 1. Settings → Locations → "Add Godown/Site"

- 2. Naam do: "Main Yard", "Site A - Patna", "Site B - Hajipur"

- 3. Har transaction mein location select karo

- 4. Location-wise report dekho

## 3.6 Barcode / QR Scanning

Kya hai? Items pe barcode lagao aur scanner se fast billing karo.

## Kise use karna chahiye?

- Interior designer jiske paas tiles, fittings, furniture items hain

- Retail store jisme bahut items hain

## Kaise use karein?

- 1. Item Master mein item ke liye barcode generate karo

- 2. Print karo → item pe chipkao

- 3. Billing mein scanner se scan karo

- 4. Item automatically add ho jaata hai — rate ke saath


## SECTION 4 — ACCOUNTING & CASH

## 4.1 Cash / Bank Entry

Kya hai? Roz ka cash aana-jaana aur bank transactions record karna.

## Kaise use karein?

- 1. Accounting → Cash/Bank Entry

- 2. Type choose: Cash receipt / Cash payment / Bank receipt / Bank payment

- 3. Party naam (optional)

- 4. Amount aur narration enter karo

- 5. Save

## Payment modes supported:

- Cash, UPI (GPay/PhonePe/Paytm), NEFT/RTGS, Cheque, DD

## 4.2 Expense Tracking

Kya hai? Labour payment, petrol, chai-paani, loading-unloading — sab business kharcha track

karo.

## Kaise use karein?

- 1. Expenses → "Add Expense"

- 2. Category choose karo: Labour / Transport / Fuel / Office / Misc

- 3. Amount enter karo

- 4. Bill ka photo attach karo (optional — proof ke liye)

- 5. Project ya site ke saath link karo

## Categories example:

- Labour wages, Supervisor salary

- Truck freight, Loading/unloading

- Petrol/diesel, Vehicle maintenance

- Site rent, Electricity

- Office stationary, Tea-coffee

## 4.3 Profit & Loss Report

Kya hai? Ek period mein kitna kamaaya aur kitna kharcha hua — net profit ya loss.


## Kaise use karein?

- 1. Reports → Profit & Loss

- 2. Date range select karo (monthly / quarterly / yearly)

- 3. Report auto-generate hogi

- 4. PDF download karo ya CA ko share karo

## Kya dikhta hai:

- Total Sales (Revenue)

- Total Purchase (Cost of Goods)

- Gross Profit = Sales - Purchase

- Total Expenses

- Net Profit = Gross Profit - Expenses

## 4.4 TDS / TCS Entry

## Kya hai?

- TDS (Tax Deducted at Source): Jab aapko payment milti hai aur buyer TDS kaatke deta hai

- TCS (Tax Collected at Source): Kuch specific goods pe seller collect karta hai

## Kise use karna chahiye?

- Construction contractors jinhein government ya large companies se payment milti hai

- ₹ 50 lakh+ turnover wale dealers

## Kaise use karein?

- 1. Invoice banao

- 2. TDS applicable check karo

- 3. Section aur percentage enter karo (e.g., Section 194C - 1%)

- 4. Amount automatically calculate hogi

- 5. TDS certificate generate karo quarter ke end mein

## 4.5 Balance Sheet

Kya hai? Aapke business ki financial position — total assets, total liabilities, owner's equity.

## Kaise use karein?

- 1. Reports → Balance Sheet

- 2. Date select karo (usually year end)


- 3. Auto-generate hogi

- 4. CA ko share karo ya khud dekho

## SECTION 5 — REPORTS & ANALYTICS

## 5.1 GST Reports (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B)

Kya hai? GST return bharne ke liye jo data chahiye — software automatically organize karta hai.

## Kaise use karein?

- 1. Reports → GST Reports

- 2. Month aur year select karo

- 3. GSTR-1 (outward sales) ya GSTR-3B (monthly return) choose karo

- 4. Excel/JSON download karo

- 5. GST portal pe upload karo ya CA ko bhejo

## Kaise kaam karta hai?

- Har invoice automatically correct GST column mein jaati hai

- B2B vs B2C sales alag-alag

- HSN summary automatically banti hai

- ITC (Input Tax Credit) track hoti hai

## 5.2 Sales Report

## Kya hai? Kitna bika, kise bika, kab bika — details ke saath.

## Filters available:

- Date range (daily / weekly / monthly / custom)

- Party-wise (kisi ek customer ki sales)

- Item-wise (kisi ek product ki sales)

- Category-wise

- Salesperson-wise

## 5.3 Day Book

Kya hai? Ek din ki saari transactions ek jagah — cash book ki tarah.

## Kaise use karein?


- 1. Reports → Day Book

- 2. Date select karo

- 3. Us din ki har entry dikhegi — invoice, payment, expense

- 4. Cash opening aur closing balance bhi dikhega

## 5.4 Business Dashboard

Kya hai? App open karte hi ek screen pe poora business summary. Kya dikhta hai:

- Aaj ki total sales Is mahine ki total sales Total outstanding (log aapka kitna dena hain) Total payable (aapko suppliers ko kitna dena hai) Low stock alerts Top 5 customers Recent transactions

- Sales vs Purchase graph

## 5.5 Project Report (Construction ke liye)

Kya hai? Har project alag — kitna material lagaa, kitni labour, kitna mila client se, kitna

profit/loss.

## Kaise use karein?

- 1. Projects → New Project banao ("Sharma House - Patna")

- 2. Isme material consumption link karo

- 3. Labour payments link karo

- 4. Client ke invoices link karo

- 5. Report mein project-wise P&L dekho

## 5.6 Cheque Management

Kya hai? Diye aur liye gaye cheques track karo — kaunsa cleared hua, kaunsa bounce hua, kaunsa pending hai.

## Kaise use karein?

- 1. Banking → Cheque Register


- 2. Cheque receive karo → entry karo

- 3. Bank mein deposit karo → "Deposited" mark karo

- 4. Cleared/Bounce notification aane par update karo

## Alerts:

- Pending cheques (bank mein diye nahi abhi)

- Due for deposit (jaldi deposit karna hai)

- Bounce alerts

## SECTION 6 — SPECIAL FEATURES (GITTI / CONSTRUCTION / INTERIOR)

## 6.1 Weight-based Billing (Gitti/Ballu ke liye)

Kya hai? Gitti, ballu, sand — ton, CFT ya Brass mein quantity enter karo, amount auto calculate

hoga.

## Kaise use karein?

- 1. Item Master mein gitti ke liye unit = "Ton" ya "CFT" ya "Brass" set karo

- 2. Rate per unit set karo (e.g., 800/ton) ₹

- 3. Invoice mein quantity enter karo

- 4. Amount automatic aayega

## Brass conversion:

- 1 Brass = 100 CFT

- 1 Brass H 4.53 Ton (depends on material)

- System mein conversion automatically ho sakti hai

## Truck slip:

- Delivery challan ke saath truck slip print hoti hai

- Vehicle number, driver naam, material, weight - sab print hota hai

- Weighbridge slip attach kar sakte ho

## 6.2 Site / Project Management

Kya hai? Multiple construction sites alag-alag track karo — har site ka alag stock, alag expense, alag billing.

## Kaise use karein?


- 1. Projects mein "New Site" banao — naam, address, client naam 2. Har material order/transfer is site se link karo 3. Har labour payment site se link karo

- 4. Site-wise report: kitna kharcha, kitna income, kitna bacha

## Example:

- Site A: Sharma House Boring Road

- Site B: Commercial Plaza Khagaul

- Dono ka alag stock, alag billing, alag P&L

## 6.3 Labour Payment & Attendance

Kya hai? Daily ya weekly mazdoor ka kaam record karo aur payment track karo. Kaise use karein?

- 1. Labour Management → Add Workers 2. Har din attendance mark karo (Present/Absent/Half day)

- 3. Rate set karo (per day / per hour) 4. Week ya month end mein payment calculate karo 5. Payment slip print karo

- 6. Amount record karo — cash ya UPI

## Report:

- Monthly attendance sheet

- Payment history per worker

- Site-wise total labour cost

## 6.4 Interior Material BOQ (Bill of Quantities)

Kya hai? Interior designer client ko deta hai ek detailed list — kaunsa material kitna lagega, kitna cost hoga.

## Kaise use karein?

- 1. New BOQ → Client naam, Project naam

- 2. Room-wise breakdown: Living Room, Bedroom, Kitchen, Bathroom

- 3. Har room mein items:

- Flooring: Tiles 60x60, 200 sqft @ 120/sqft = 24,000 ₹ ₹

- Wall: Paint 2 coats, 800 sqft @ ¹ 18/sqft = ¹ 14,400


- Ceiling: False ceiling, 200 sqft @ ¹ 95/sqft = ¹ 19,000

- 4. Labour charges alag se add karo

- 5. Total BOQ PDF share karo client ko

- 6. Approve hone par invoice convert karo

## Units interior ke liye:

- Sqft (flooring, wall, ceiling)

- Rft (running feet  skirting, dado)

- Nos (doors, windows, fixtures)

- Lumpsum (complete work packages)

## 6.5 Photo Attach (Work Proof)

Kya hai? Bill ke saath kaam ki photo attach karo — professional dikhai deta hai aur proof rehta

hai.

## Kaise use karein?

- 1. Invoice ya challan banao

- 2. "Attach Photo" button dabao

- 3. Camera se kheeecho ya gallery se upload karo

- 4. Multiple photos attach ho sakti hain

- 5. Client ko PDF bhejte waqt photos bhi jaati hain

## Kab useful hai:

- Interior designer: before/after photos with final bill

- Contractor: work progress photos ke saath invoice

- Delivery: truck mein loaded material ki photo challan ke saath
